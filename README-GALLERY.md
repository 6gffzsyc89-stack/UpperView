## The brief

> The gallery section is not clear that people can swipe through images. Is there a way you can make this more obvious? Several inquiries have been made. Should there be just a gallery link of some kind? Look at the mobile versions of Nobu, W Hotel and others that were the basis of the Upperview website design. How can we make it clear that the images are slidable for the rooms and the villas? Do you recommend showing thumbnails, very small ones, and that as you slide left and right, the thumbnail that's being displayed is also highlighted? That's a traditional desktop style control. I have seen this sometimes as pop-ups on a mobile, like you click the image and you can see that almost like a film strip under the main image as you slide with your finger left and right an image in the center is getting highlighted. I'm not sure if that's the best. So what I want you to do is look at how others do it. Maybe we just have a little bit of text to say you can slide, you know, left and right through the images, something like that, or click the mouse if you can detect it's a desktop. And actually maybe that's purely based on the resolution, right? So we can tell the device if we tell them to click or we can tell them to slide this sort of thing or just both you make the decision about this just make it obvious other to prevent users from saying, do you have any more photos of the rooms and the villas? Which is a common question that is being asked at on the current Upper View website? Make the change once you've done enough research.
