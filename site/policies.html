Cancellation & Refund Policy

← Upper View

Applies to reservations made directly on upperviewsamui.com. Reservations made through Booking.com, Airbnb, Agoda or Trip.com follow the cancellation terms shown on that platform at the time of booking.

Stays of up to 27 nights

Free cancellation — cancel at least 30 days before check-in for a full refund.
50% refund — cancel at least 7 days before check-in for a refund of half the total paid.
Non-refundable — cancellations within 7 days of check-in, and no-shows, are not refunded.

In addition, any booking made more than 7 days before check-in may be cancelled free of charge within 24 hours of booking, whatever the dates.

Stays of 28 nights or more

Long stays follow a stricter policy: full refund within 48 hours of booking (when check-in is at least 28 days away); after that, the first 30 nights are non-refundable. Please write to us before booking a long stay if you have questions.

Refunds

Refunds are returned to the original payment card. Processing typically takes 5–10 business days depending on your bank. Taxes and fees included in the booking are refunded in the same proportion as the room amount.

How to cancel

Reply to your confirmation email, or write to booking@upperviewsamui.com quoting your confirmation number (UV-…). The date we receive your message is the cancellation date.

Check-in & check-out

Check-in from 3:00 PM · Check-out by 12:00 PM (hotel local time, GMT+7). All times in this policy are hotel local time.
